### 🛡️ [Human Slop – Anti-AI Social Platform](https://humanslop.in) *(2024 – 2026)*
> **Co-Founder & Lead Engineer** · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.humanslop.app) · [Website](https://humanslop.in)

> A privacy-first, authenticity-first social platform where every post is typed manually — no copy-paste, no AI shortcuts. Real-time typing forensics generate a **Human Score** to reward organic writing over synthetic content.

- **Biometric Integrity**: Hardware-bound biometric authentication to ensure account uniqueness and prevent bot farming at the device level.
- **Typing Forensics**: Real-time behavioral analysis (WPM, burst patterns, inter-keystroke intervals) computing a **Human Score** — systematically filtering LLM-generated content.
- **Architecture**: High-availability **dual-database architecture** (PostgreSQL + AWS) separating encrypted user PII from social graph data.
- **Deployment**: Fully deployed **web + Android app** on Google Play, hosting a community dedicated to authentic, AI-free human interactions.
- **YC Startup School**: Accepted into **Y Combinator Startup School** for Human Slop.
